In Park City, your rental is competing with hundreds of others on Airbnb and VRBO, and guests decide in a few seconds of scrolling. The first photo, the photo order, and the amenities you show make the difference between a booking and a skip. We shoot vacation rentals the same way we shoot luxury listings: bright, true to color, and focused on what guests are paying for.

## Where we shoot vacation rentals

We photograph rentals across [Park City](/real-estate-photography/park-city/), including Old Town, Deer Valley, Park Meadows, Kimball Junction, Jeremy Ranch, and Pinebrook, as well as Heber, Midway, Kamas, and the cabins and condos of [Ogden Valley](/real-estate-photography/ogden-valley/) near Powder Mountain and Snowbasin. We work with individual owners and with property managers who need consistent photos across many units.

## What guests want to see

Guests booking a mountain rental want to see the hot tub, the fireplace, the view from the deck, the ski storage, the game room, and how many people the beds really fit. We plan every shoot around those features and deliver photos in an order that tells the story, starting with the strongest image for your thumbnail.

Inside, we bracket and blend every shot so rooms look bright and natural, and the mountains still show through the windows. We shoot wide but natural angles so rooms don't look stretched or misleading, which keeps reviews honest and guests happy.

## Drone aerials for rentals

Location is one of the biggest reasons guests choose a Park City rental. Drone photos show how close the home is to the lifts, the trails, and Main Street, and they show the setting in a way no ground photo can. Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot through our sister company, [801 Aerial]({{ site.sister_brand_url }}), and we never fly over crowds or busy ski runs.

## Video and social media for rentals

A short video tour and a vertical reel help your listing stand out and give you content for Instagram, Facebook, and your direct booking site. If you manage several rentals, our [social media management](/services/social-media-management/) can keep your pages active with new posts every week.

## Scheduling around guests

We schedule around your turnover window, and most rental shoots take one to two hours. Winter shoots with snow can be a big draw for ski season bookings, and some owners book a winter and a summer shoot so their listing always matches the season. Edited photos are delivered within 24–48 hours.

In Utah, the view often sells the home. Drone photography shows what ground photos can't: the size of the lot, the backyard and pool from above, how close the home sits to trails and parks, and the mountains behind it.

All aerial work is flown by our sister company, [801 Aerial](https://801aerial.com), with an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot. For land and lot listings, we can add property line overlays so buyers see exactly what they're getting.

## Drone photography in Ogden and Weber County

We fly listings across [Ogden](/real-estate-photography/ogden/), North Ogden, Pleasant View, and up into [Ogden Valley](/real-estate-photography/ogden-valley/), including Huntsville, Eden, and Liberty. Aerials show the view of Ben Lomond, the land around cabins and custom builds, and how close a home is to Pineview Reservoir, Snowbasin, and Powder Mountain. Parts of Ogden sit under controlled airspace around Ogden-Hinckley Airport and Hill Air Force Base, so we check every address and get FAA authorization before the flight when it's required.

## Drone photography in Park City

In [Park City](/real-estate-photography/park-city/) and the rest of Summit County, aerial photos and video show how close a home is to the lifts, the trails, and Main Street, and they show the acreage around properties in Promontory, Silver Creek, and the Kamas Valley. We plan mountain flights around wind and weather, and we never fly over crowds or busy ski runs.

## Drone photography across the Wasatch Front

We also fly real estate listings in [Salt Lake City](/real-estate-photography/salt-lake-city/), [Draper](/real-estate-photography/draper/), [Farmington](/real-estate-photography/farmington/), and all of Utah County, including [Lehi](/real-estate-photography/lehi/), [Alpine](/real-estate-photography/alpine/), [Pleasant Grove](/real-estate-photography/pleasant-grove/), [Orem](/real-estate-photography/orem/), and [Provo](/real-estate-photography/provo/). Aerial photos and video are included in Full Listing Media, starting at $400.

Orem is full of family homes with big yards, mature trees, and clear views of Mount Timpanogos. We make sure buyers see all of it: bright interiors, a strong curb shot, and aerials that show the yard, the street, and nearby parks and schools.

## Neighborhoods we shoot in Orem

From Orem's east bench to the neighborhoods near Utah Valley University, we shoot listings of every size. That includes Cascade, Northridge, Windsor, Sharon, Orchard, Suncrest, Sunset Heights, and Geneva Heights, plus condos and townhomes near University Place. We also cover Vineyard, Lindon, and Provo, all with no travel fee anywhere in Utah County.

## What Orem listings need

Many Orem homes were built in the 1960s through the 1990s and have been updated over the years. Our job is to show off those updates. We bracket and blend every interior shot so kitchens, bathrooms, and living spaces look bright and clean, and we use wide but natural angles so rooms don't look stretched.

Orem buyers are often families, so the yard matters. Aerials show the size of the lot, the garden or play area, and how close the home is to parks, schools, and the Murdock Canal Trail. On the east bench, we frame exteriors and drone shots toward Mount Timpanogos.

## Rentals, condos, and investment properties

With UVU nearby, many Orem listings are rentals or investment properties. For those, we keep the shoot fast and practical: clean, bright photos that show every room, delivered quickly so you can list right away. Listing photos start at $250.

## Video tours and social media

For family homes and higher-end listings, a video tour and a vertical reel help buyers feel the space before they visit. We can post your listings to Instagram and Facebook as part of our social media management, so your marketing keeps working after the sign goes up.

Orem is about 10 minutes from our home base in Pleasant Grove, so scheduling is easy. Send the address and your listing date, and we'll confirm a time within 24 hours. Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours of the shoot.

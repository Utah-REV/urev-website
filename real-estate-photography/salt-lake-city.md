Salt Lake City listings range from historic bungalows in Sugar House and the Avenues to East Bench homes with valley views and downtown condos. Older homes often have smaller windows and darker rooms, so we bracket and blend every shot to keep rooms bright and true to color without looking fake.

## Neighborhoods we shoot in Salt Lake City

We shoot across Salt Lake City, including Sugar House, the Avenues, Federal Heights, Yalecrest, the East Bench, Liberty Wells, 9th and 9th, Rose Park, and downtown. We also cover Millcreek, Holladay, Cottonwood Heights, Murray, Sandy, and Draper, so agents working anywhere in Salt Lake County can use one team.

## Historic homes and bungalows

Many Salt Lake homes are 80 to 100 years old, with original woodwork, built-ins, and smaller rooms. We shoot them to show the character without making the rooms look cramped: careful angles, bright and natural editing, and detail shots of the features buyers love in older homes.

## Condos and downtown listings

For condos and townhomes, we focus on light, layout, and the building amenities. City and mountain views from the windows are part of the value, so we make sure the view shows up in the photos instead of blowing out white.

## Drone photography in Salt Lake City

Aerials are especially useful on the East Bench and in the foothills, where the view of the valley is part of what buyers are paying for. Much of Salt Lake City sits under the controlled airspace around Salt Lake City International Airport. Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot, and we check every address and get FAA authorization before the shoot when it's required.

## Video and social media

Our Full Listing Media package includes photos, drone, a walkthrough video tour, and a vertical reel from one visit. We can also manage your Instagram and Facebook, posting your listings and keeping your page active between sales.

## Pricing and travel

Listing photos start at $250, and full listing media with drone and video starts at $400. A small travel fee may apply outside Utah County, so send the address for an exact quote. Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours of the shoot.

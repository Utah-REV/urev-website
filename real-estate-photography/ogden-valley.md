Ogden Valley is one of the most beautiful places in Utah to sell a home. Between Pineview Reservoir, Snowbasin, Powder Mountain, and the mountains on every side, buyers here are paying for the setting as much as the house. We've filmed custom builds and shops in Huntsville and Eden, and we know how to show the property and everything around it.

## Areas we shoot in Ogden Valley

We cover Huntsville, Eden, and Liberty, along with the homes and cabins around Pineview Reservoir, Wolf Creek, Nordic Valley, and the roads up to Snowbasin and Powder Mountain. We also shoot down the canyon in [Ogden](/real-estate-photography/ogden/), North Ogden, and Pleasant View, and over the mountain in Park City and the Wasatch Back.

## Drone photography and aerial video in the valley

In Ogden Valley, a drone is the best way to show what a property really offers. Aerial photos show the acreage, the reservoir, the ski runs, and how the home sits against the mountains. Aerial video gives buyers a sense of the drive in, the views from the deck, and how close they'll be to the lake and the resorts. For land and acreage listings, we can add property line overlays so buyers see exactly what's included.

Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot through our sister company, [801 Aerial]({{ site.sister_brand_url }}). We plan flights around wind and weather, which can change fast in the mountains, and we check every address before the shoot.

## Cabins, custom homes, and shops

Many Ogden Valley listings are cabins, second homes, and custom builds with big garages and shops for boats, snowmobiles, and gear. We photograph the features these buyers care about: great rooms with tall windows, mudrooms and ski storage, decks and hot tubs, and shop space. Inside, we bracket and blend every shot so rooms are bright and the views still show through the windows.

## Vacation rentals

Homes near Powder Mountain, Wolf Creek, and Pineview often double as vacation rentals. We shoot rental listings for Airbnb and VRBO the same way we shoot for the MLS, with bright interiors, the amenities guests look for, and aerials that show how close the home is to the lake and the slopes.

## Video and social media

Our Full Listing Media package includes photos, drone, a walkthrough video tour, and a vertical reel from one visit. We can also post your listings to Instagram and Facebook as part of our [social media management](/services/social-media-management/), so every home you sell in the valley keeps marketing your business.

## Pricing and scheduling

Listing photos start at $250, and full listing media with drone and video starts at $400. A travel fee applies for Weber County, so send the address for an exact quote. In winter, we plan shoots around snow and road conditions, and snowy exteriors can be a big selling point for ski-area homes. Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours of the shoot.

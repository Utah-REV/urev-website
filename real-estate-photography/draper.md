Draper homes, from SunCrest up on the mountain to the neighborhoods around Corner Canyon and South Mountain, often come with big views over the valley. We make the view part of the story: exteriors framed toward the valley, aerials that show the setting, and interiors shot so the windows look out on the scenery instead of blowing out white.

## Neighborhoods we shoot in Draper

We cover all of Draper, including SunCrest, Corner Canyon, South Mountain, Draper Heights, Hidden Valley, and the newer homes near Point of the Mountain and the east bench. We also shoot in nearby Sandy, Riverton, Herriman, Bluffdale, and Lehi, so agents working the south end of Salt Lake County and the north end of Utah County can book one team.

## What Draper listings need

Many Draper homes sit on hillsides, which makes them hard to photograph from the street. A drone fixes that. Aerials show the whole home, the lot, and the view across the valley, and they show buyers how close the home is to the Corner Canyon trails, which is a big selling point in Draper.

Hillside homes also tend to have walls of windows facing the valley. We bracket and blend every interior shot so the room is bright and the view outside still shows up. For homes up in SunCrest, we plan around wind and weather, since the mountain can be very different from the valley floor.

## Video tours and reels

Draper buyers are often relocating for work along the Silicon Slopes corridor, and many tour homes online before they ever visit. A video tour and a short vertical reel help them get a feel for the layout and the setting. We can also post the reel to your Instagram and Facebook as part of our social media management.

American Fork has a little of everything: older homes with character near Main Street, family neighborhoods with big yards, and newer builds on the north bench with views of Mount Timpanogos and the mouth of American Fork Canyon. We shoot all of it, and since we're based next door in Pleasant Grove, American Fork listings are easy to fit into the schedule.

## Neighborhoods we shoot in American Fork

We cover all of American Fork, from the established neighborhoods around Main Street and the historic center to the newer homes on the north bench toward Highland and Cedar Hills, and the neighborhoods on the west side near I-15 and Utah Lake. We also shoot in nearby [Lehi](/real-estate-photography/lehi/), [Pleasant Grove](/real-estate-photography/pleasant-grove/), [Alpine](/real-estate-photography/alpine/), Highland, and Cedar Hills, with no travel fee anywhere in Utah County.

## What American Fork listings need

Older American Fork homes have often been updated over the years, and our job is to show off those updates. We bracket and blend every interior shot so kitchens, bathrooms, and living spaces look bright and true to color, with wide but honest angles that don't make rooms look stretched.

On the north bench, the mountains are part of what buyers are paying for. We frame exteriors and drone shots toward Mount Timpanogos and the canyon, and aerials show the size of the lot, the backyard, and how close the home is to parks, schools, and trails.

## New construction and builders

American Fork and the surrounding cities have a steady stream of new homes. We work with builders who need consistent photos across a community, keeping the same angles, lighting, and editing style from home to home so a whole subdivision looks polished online.

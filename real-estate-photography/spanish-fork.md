Spanish Fork listings range from new homes on the west side to view homes and building lots on the east bench, below the mountains and the wind turbines at the mouth of Spanish Fork Canyon. On the bench, the setting is a big part of what sells, so drone photos do a lot of the work.

## Lots and land in Spanish Fork

Vacant lots are hard to sell with ground photos. A grassy slope looks the same as every other grassy slope. We fly the lot from several heights and angles, then draw the property lines on the photos so buyers can see the boundary, the neighbors, the street access, and the view. A short top-down drone clip shows the shape of the lot in seconds.

## Homes on the east bench

For view homes, we shoot the front when the light is best and add aerials that show the mountains behind and the valley in front. Interiors are bracketed and blended so rooms look bright and natural, and windows still show the view.

## Drone photos in Spanish Fork

We've filmed several custom builds in Farmington, including a barn-style estate, and we know how to show off both the home and the land around it.

## Areas we shoot in Davis County

Along with Farmington, we shoot throughout Davis County, including Kaysville, Fruit Heights, Centerville, Bountiful, Layton, and Syracuse. Whether the listing is a starter home near Station Park, a family home on the Farmington bench, or a larger property on the west side near Farmington Bay, we tailor the shoot to what will sell it.

## What Farmington listings need

Bench homes in Farmington and Fruit Heights usually have views west over the lake and valley, so we time exterior and drone shots for the best light on the view. Larger properties with acreage, shops, or barns need aerials to show the whole parcel, the outbuildings, and how the land is laid out. For land and lot listings, we can add property line overlays so buyers see exactly what's included.

Inside, we bracket and blend every frame so rooms look bright and natural, and we shoot the features that matter in this area: mudrooms, big garages, shop space, and outdoor living areas.

## Drone notes for Davis County

Parts of Davis County sit close to Hill Air Force Base and its controlled airspace. Every drone flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot, and we check every address ahead of time and get FAA authorization when it's needed, so the aerial part of your shoot happens on schedule and legally.

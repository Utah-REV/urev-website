Saratoga Springs is one of the fastest-growing cities in Utah, and much of it is new construction. When dozens of similar homes are listed at the same time, strong photos and video are how a listing stands out. We shoot homes across Saratoga Springs and neighboring Eagle Mountain for agents and builders.

## Areas we shoot in Saratoga Springs

We cover all of Saratoga Springs, including Wildflower, Jacobs Ranch, Harvest Hills, and the homes along the west shore of Utah Lake near Inlet Park. We also shoot in Eagle Mountain, [Lehi](/real-estate-photography/lehi/), Bluffdale, and [American Fork](/real-estate-photography/american-fork/), with no travel fee anywhere in Utah County.

## Making a new build stand out

Newer homes in Saratoga Springs often share floor plans with the homes around them. We focus on what makes each listing different: upgraded kitchens, finished basements, landscaped yards, and views. Every interior is bracketed and blended so rooms look bright and true to color, and exteriors are timed for the best light on the front of the home.

## Aerials of lake views and open space

Many Saratoga Springs buyers are paying for the setting: views across Utah Lake, mountain views to the east, and parks, trails, and open space nearby. Drone photos show all of that in one image, along with the lot and how the home sits in the neighborhood.

## Builders and model homes

We work with builders who need consistent media across a community, keeping the same angles, lighting, and editing style from home to home. Model homes and Parade of Homes entries get the full treatment, including video tours and reels for social media.

## Video, social media, and pricing

Our Full Listing Media package includes photos, drone, a walkthrough video tour, and a vertical reel from one visit, starting at $400. Listing photos start at $250. We can also post your listings to Instagram and Facebook as part of our [social media management](/services/social-media-management/). Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours of the shoot.

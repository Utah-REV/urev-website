Park City listings sell a lifestyle as much as a home. Whether it's a mountain home, a condo near the slopes, or a property with acreage, buyers want to see the setting, and many are shopping from out of state. That makes strong photos, aerials, and a video tour essential.

## Areas we shoot in Park City and Summit County

We shoot throughout Park City and Summit County, including Old Town, Deer Valley, Park Meadows, Thaynes Canyon, Jeremy Ranch, Pinebrook, Kimball Junction, Silver Creek, and Promontory. We also cover the Heber Valley, Midway, and Kamas for agents working across the Wasatch Back.

## Drone photography and aerial video in Park City

In Park City, the location is a big part of the price. Drone photos and aerial video show how close a home is to the lifts, the trails, and Main Street, and they show the mountain views from the deck or the hot tub. For larger properties in Promontory, Silver Creek, or the Kamas Valley, aerials show the acreage, the access, and the land around the home.

Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot through our sister company, 801 Aerial. We plan flights around wind and weather, which can change quickly in the mountains, and we don't fly over crowds or busy ski runs. Aerial video is included in our Full Listing Media package and can be cut into a vertical reel for social media.

## Photographing mountain homes

Mountain homes usually have big windows, high ceilings, timber, and stone. We bracket and blend every shot so the interior is bright and the view outside still shows through the windows. We also photograph the features out-of-state buyers look for: ski rooms, mudrooms, garages, hot tubs, and outdoor living spaces.

## Winter and summer shoots

We plan Park City shoots around light and weather, and we're happy to schedule winter shoots that show off the snow. For homes that will sell in both seasons, some agents book a winter and a summer shoot so the listing always looks current.

## Pricing and travel

Listing photos start at $250, and full listing media with drone and video starts at $400. A travel fee applies for Summit and Wasatch counties, so send the address for an exact quote. Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours.

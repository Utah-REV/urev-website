Lehi is one of the fastest-growing cities in Utah, which means a lot of new construction and a lot of competition. Neighborhoods like Traverse Mountain and Holbrook Farms, and the area around Thanksgiving Point and Silicon Slopes, are full of homes that look similar online. Strong photos are how your listing stands out.

## Neighborhoods we shoot in Lehi

We shoot all over Lehi, including Traverse Mountain, Holbrook Farms, Ivory Ridge, Cold Spring Ranch, the homes around Thanksgiving Point, and the older streets of downtown Lehi. We also cover nearby Saratoga Springs, Eagle Mountain, American Fork, Highland, and Draper, all with no travel fee inside Utah County.

## Resale homes and new builds

We shoot both resale homes and new builds, and we work with builders who need consistent media across many homes in a community. For builders, we keep the same angles, lighting, and editing style from home to home, so a whole community looks polished and consistent on your website and the MLS.

For resale homes, the goal is to stand out from the newer builds nearby. That means bright, true-to-color interiors, a strong front exterior, and twilight or golden-hour shots when the home has curb appeal worth showing off.

## Why aerials matter in Lehi

Lehi buyers care a lot about location: how far the home is from I-15, Silicon Slopes offices, schools, parks, and the trail system. Drone photos show all of that in one image. On Traverse Mountain, aerials also show the views over Utah Lake and the valley that buyers are paying extra for.

## Video and social media

Many Lehi buyers are relocating for tech jobs and tour homes online first. A walkthrough video tour helps them understand the layout, and a vertical reel gets your listing seen on Instagram and Facebook. As part of our social media management, we can post your listing content for you so every home you sell builds your brand.

Ogden has some of the most character-rich homes in northern Utah, from historic houses on the east bench to newer builds with views of Ben Lomond. We shoot listings across Ogden, North Ogden, and up into Ogden Valley, including Huntsville and Eden, where we have filmed custom builds.

## Areas we shoot in Ogden and Weber County

We cover all of Ogden, including the east bench, the historic homes near Jefferson Avenue and downtown, and the neighborhoods off Harrison Boulevard. We also shoot in North Ogden, Pleasant View, South Ogden, Washington Terrace, Riverdale, and Roy, and up the canyon in Ogden Valley, including Huntsville, Eden, and Liberty near Pineview Reservoir, Snowbasin, and Powder Mountain.

## Drone photography and aerial video in Ogden

Drone work is a big part of selling homes in Ogden and Weber County. In North Ogden and on the east bench, aerials show the view of Ben Lomond and the mountains behind the home. In Ogden Valley, they show the land, the reservoir, and how close a cabin or custom build is to the ski resorts. For land and acreage listings, we can add property line overlays so buyers see the exact parcel.

Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 licensed pilot through our sister company, 801 Aerial. Parts of Ogden sit under controlled airspace around Ogden-Hinckley Airport and Hill Air Force Base, so we check every address ahead of time and get FAA authorization when it's required. Aerial video is included in our Full Listing Media package and can be cut into a vertical reel for social media.

## Photographing historic and newer homes

Older Ogden homes often have smaller windows, darker woodwork, and lots of character. We bracket and blend every shot so rooms are bright and true to color without losing the original details. Newer homes in North Ogden and the valley get wide, clean interiors and twilight exteriors when the view is worth it.

## Pricing and travel

Listing photos start at $250, and full listing media with drone aerials and a video tour starts at $400. A small travel fee may apply for Weber County, so send the address for an exact quote. Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours of the shoot.

Alpine is known for custom luxury homes on big lots below Lone Peak, and it's where we've shot some of our favorite work, including a Parade of Homes winner. Homes like these need more than a standard shoot: wide aerials of the lot and views, careful interior lighting, and video that matches the price point.

## Neighborhoods we shoot in Alpine

We shoot across all of Alpine, from the homes along Alpine Boulevard and Main Street to the larger estates near Lambert Park, Box Elder, and the foothills below Lone Peak and the Alpine Cove area. We also cover nearby Highland, Cedar Hills, and the north end of American Fork, so agents working the north end of Utah County can use one photographer for every listing.

## What Alpine listings need

Most Alpine buyers are paying for space, privacy, and the mountain setting. A ground photo can't show a one-acre lot, a pool, a sport court, or how the home sits against the mountains, so we plan the drone shots first. Top-down aerials show the yard and pool layout, and higher shots show the canyon and Lone Peak behind the home.

Inside, custom homes usually have tall windows, mixed lighting, and dark wood or stone. We bracket and blend every frame so the rooms look bright and true to color while the windows still show the view outside. Home theaters, sport courts, and wine rooms get their own shots, because those are the features buyers remember.

## Video and social for luxury listings

For homes at this price, we recommend Full Listing Media: photos, drone, a walkthrough video tour, and a vertical reel from the same visit. The reel is built for Instagram and Facebook, and we can post it for you as part of our social media management, so your listing reaches buyers beyond the MLS.

## Scheduling in Alpine

Alpine is about 15 minutes from our home base in Pleasant Grove, so there's no travel fee and scheduling is easy. For homes on the east side of town we usually shoot exteriors in the morning, before the sun drops behind the mountains in the afternoon. Edited photos and video are delivered within 24–48 hours.

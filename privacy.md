*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

Utah Real Estate Visuals ("UREV," "we," "us") is based in Pleasant Grove, Utah. This policy explains what information we collect through utahrealestatevisuals.com, our booking forms, and our Facebook and Instagram lead forms, and how we use it.

## What we collect

- **Information you give us:** your name, email, phone number, brokerage, property address, and anything else you include when you book a shoot or fill out one of our forms.
- **Usage information:** pages visited, the ad or link that brought you here, and basic device and browser details, collected through Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel.

## How we use it

- To reply to your request, schedule shoots, and send quotes
- To provide the photography, video, drone, and social media services you ask for
- To understand which pages and ads are working, so we can improve them

We do not sell your personal information.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run the business, such as our form provider (Formspree), Google Analytics, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and only as needed to provide our services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

## Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Email [utahrev@gmail.com](mailto:utahrev@gmail.com) or call or text 801-900-1713.

## Cookies

Our site uses cookies from Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure visits and ad performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

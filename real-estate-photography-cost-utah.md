## What changes the price

- **Size of the home.** A large custom home takes longer to light and photograph than a condo, and it usually needs more photos to show every space.
- **Travel.** There's no travel fee anywhere in Utah County. Listings in Salt Lake, Davis, Weber, Summit, and Wasatch counties may have a small travel fee.
- **Drone and video.** Aerials and a video tour add time on site and in editing, which is why they're part of the Full Listing Media package.
- **Add-ons.** Rush delivery, extra reels, and property line overlays for land listings are priced separately.

## Who pays for listing photos

In Utah, the listing agent usually pays for professional photography as part of marketing the home. Some agents pass the cost to the seller, or split it on high-end listings. We invoice whoever books the shoot.

## Is professional photography worth the cost?

For almost every listing, yes. Photos are the first thing buyers see on the MLS, Zillow, and social media, and they decide which homes get a showing. A $250 shoot is a small cost compared to the price of a home, and bright, true-to-color photos help a listing stand out from ones taken on a phone. For homes where the lot, views, or location sell the property, drone aerials and video are worth adding.
